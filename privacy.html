Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 12, 2025

Overview

Acconecto ("we", "our", "us") operates the FormLab mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

FormLab is designed with privacy in mind. All pose analysis is performed entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Camera footage or images
  • Body pose data or movement patterns
  • Workout session data
  • Personal health information

On-Device Processing

All body pose estimation and form analysis happens locally on your iPhone. No video, image, or pose data ever leaves your device. Your workout history is stored locally using on-device storage and is never uploaded to any server.

Camera Usage

FormLab requires camera access to perform real-time pose estimation. Camera data is processed in real-time and is not recorded or stored unless you explicitly choose to record a session. Recorded videos are saved to your device's local storage and optionally to your photo library.

Third-Party Services

FormLab may include Google AdMob for displaying advertisements in the free version. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data according to Google's privacy policy. Pro users who remove ads are not subject to any ad-related data collection.

Data Storage

All workout data (session history, rep counts, form scores) is stored locally on your device using SQLite. This data is not synced, backed up to any cloud service, or accessible to us in any way.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at acconecto@gmail.com.
