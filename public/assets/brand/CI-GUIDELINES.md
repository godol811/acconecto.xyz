# Aconecto CI Guidelines

## Brand idea

The bridge-shaped `A` represents connection and the full product journey: idea, interface, engineering, and launch. The violet structure carries the forward motion; the near-black bridge completes the form.

## Primary colors

| Token | HEX | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aconecto Violet | `#6847F5` | Primary brand accent and symbol |
| Deep Violet | `#4B2AD8` | Supporting violet tone |
| Aconecto Black | `#111318` | Wordmark, body text, dark surfaces |
| Warm Ivory | `#F5F2EC` | Preferred light background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Reversed logo and clear space |

## Typography

- English and UI: Inter, weights 400–700
- Korean and UI: Pretendard, weights 400–700
- Use tight but readable headings. Do not stretch, condense, or outline the wordmark.

## Logo variants

| File | Intended use |
| --- | --- |
| `aconecto-lockup-color.png` | Primary logo on white or warm-ivory backgrounds |
| `aconecto-lockup-white.png` | Dark backgrounds, photos, and hero sections |
| `aconecto-lockup-black.png` | One-color documents and print |
| `aconecto-symbol-color.png` | App icons, avatars, badges, and square placements |
| `aconecto-symbol-white.png` | One-color reversed symbol |
| `aconecto-symbol-black.png` | One-color light-background symbol |
| `aconecto-icon-32.png` to `aconecto-icon-512.png` | Favicons and web-app metadata |

## Clear space and minimum size

- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the width of the black bridge stroke.
- Minimum digital width: 120 px for the horizontal lockup and 24 px for the symbol.
- Minimum print width: 32 mm for the horizontal lockup and 8 mm for the symbol.
- At small sizes, use the standalone symbol rather than compressing the wordmark.

## Background use

- Use the color logo on white or warm ivory.
- Use the white logo on near-black, violet, photographs, or visually busy backgrounds.
- Maintain strong contrast. Do not place the black wordmark directly on dark imagery.

## Do not

- Change the relationship or spacing between the symbol and wordmark.
- Rotate, skew, stretch, add shadows, or introduce unrelated colors.
- Put the logo inside an arbitrary shape unless it is an approved app-icon container.
- Recreate the wordmark with a substitute typeface when the supplied lockup is available.
